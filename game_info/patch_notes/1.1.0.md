# Patch 1.1
> New hero: Polypus !!!

> This patch mainly focuses on the new content, a bigger balance patch and polishing update will follow!

## Features:
- New hero: Polypus
  - Increased chance for Ancient encounters
- New quests:
  - Mourning: After 8 friendly units get destroyed in combat, get a graveyard.
  - Fatigued: After round 1, 2 and 3, get a Haunted Clocktower and Spirit Torch.
  - Sacrificial Pact: After permanently destroying a unit, get a random tier 1 Ancient (6 times).
  - Rebound: When you lose 50 Health after combat, gain back 30.
  - Keep on Digging: Start the game with the Hole.
- New solo adventure for Polypus

## Bugs:
- Fix dialogue in the SNAIL solo adventure
- Fix emergus dialogue box in solo adventure to use updated art

## Other:
- Zapzap, Auris and Ambrose solo adventures now use their islands.
